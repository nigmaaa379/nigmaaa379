### 🧠 The Mission: Why FinMind?

Remote professionals are earning more but feeling poorer and exhausted. I'm building **FinMind** to fix the "Bleeding Neck" problems:
- 💸 **Financial Optimization:** AI agents that negotiate bills and find hidden savings.
- 🛑 **Boundary Protection:** Auto-responses to protect personal time from work creep.
- 🧘 **Mental Health:** Behavioral analysis to prevent burnout before it happens.

> *"People don't need another AI tool. They need an AI that works **for** them, not the other way around."*
